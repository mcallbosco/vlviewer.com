It's finally time for the long-awaited Deadlock update. If you've used the site before, you know I'm usually pretty quick with updates. If you're new, here's how things will likely go once the update drops.

## Hour 1 (or Less): The Initial Drop

Most of the new content should be on the site within an hour. If everything goes smoothly, I'm hoping for closer to 30 minutes.

Things may be a little disorganized at first, especially if Valve has changed how files are named or added new categories.

## Hour 2: Cleanup

Once the initial batch is live, I'll look for lines my automated scans missed, including anything using a new naming scheme. I'll also tidy up new categories and make other refinements.

## Hour 3 and Throughout the Night: Transcript Corrections

I'll go through the new voice lines and clean up incorrect transcripts. Valve's official transcript file probably won't cover everything, so I'll still need speech-to-text software to fill in the gaps.

You can help too! If you spot an incorrect transcript with an edit option, you can submit a correction directly on the site. The new editor also lets you group recordings that share the same dialogue and mark non-speech sounds that were mistakenly transcribed as words. You can read more in the **A New Transcript Editor** section of the [VLViewer 2.0 post](/blog/2026-9VLViewer2).

## What If Valve Adds More Voice Languages?

If the update includes voice acting in additional languages, I'll try to get those up the same night. That may take longer, though, since I don't have a system set up for it yet.

Either way, adding other languages won't delay getting the English voice lines onto the site.

When the update lands, head to the [Deadlock voice lines page](https://deadlock.vlviewer.com/voicelines/) and keep **Show New voice lines on top** enabled to find the new additions. You can also use the [Patch Explorer](https://deadlock.vlviewer.com/patches/) to compare versions and see what's been added, changed, or removed.
